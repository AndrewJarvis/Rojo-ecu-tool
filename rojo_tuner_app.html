Logs

Log live data records engine speed, throttle, temperatures, battery, O2, the fuel factor setting and the rest as a CSV file - press it to start, press again to stop and save. The injection quantity and lambda/O2 correction columns move with the throttle; their exact meaning and units are best guesses, and m5_acc is not identified. Log everything saves one text file for diagnosis: the ECU details, current settings and fault codes, a fresh raw snapshot, and every line exchanged with the Arduino since you connected.

Fast self-learning

–
–
From the original app: fast-learning lets the engine reach its best state in the shortest time. Do it with the bike stationary and unloaded - never on the road - and with the engine fully warm (cylinder head temperature in the original app's green zone); it may stop the engine. Once started, the ECU decides when you are "in a learning point". Change engine speed or throttle slowly and steadily until the status says you are in a point, hold it until that point is learned, then look for the next. A valid O2 sensor reading is needed (0 mV means invalid). When all points are done, switch the ignition off. The original app does not publish the target speed or throttle for each point.
